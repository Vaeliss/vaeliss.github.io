About

I am a PhD candidate working on robust machine learning for physiological and health data, with sleep as the main application domain.

My research studies models under real-world heterogeneity: subject variability, device differences, dataset shifts, multimodal sensing constraints, and noisy labels. The broader goal is to build health AI systems that can be evaluated fairly and transferred across data sources.

I am interested in future R&D roles around wearable sensing, physiological time series, robust generalization, multimodal modeling, and research-to-code execution.
