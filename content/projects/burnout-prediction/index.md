This work contributes to an Innosuisse work-related stress disorder project, including data collection and model development for burnout detection from passive wearable data.

The modeling work explores burnout severity estimation from wearable trajectories plus demographic and occupational information, using classification, regression, and ranking-oriented methods for physiological risk monitoring.
