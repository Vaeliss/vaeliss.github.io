This project studies passive daily monitoring of perceived sleep quality in free-living conditions.

The modeling work combines wearable and self-report data, subject-wise validation, and individual-baseline features. A central finding is that deviations from personal baselines are important predictors for population-level perceived sleep quality modeling.
